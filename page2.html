About Us

OUR BACKGROUND

Leaf & Sip comes from a combination of sipping your favourite drinks in a garden with a peaceful environment, perfect when you want to escape from a hectic life. The founders, 4 cafe hunters has been searching for the perfect cafe that could showcase the beauty of the nature with a combination of modern cafe. With a great support and advises from families, 'Leaf & Sip' is successfully established on 30/3/2026.

OUR INSPIRATION 

Our biggest inspiration is creating a safe space for cafe lovers to enjoy sipping their drinks while healing with nature and greenery environment. The diversity of our customers, from kids, students, senior citizens, families, are all welcome in our safe haven. Your enjoyment and smiles are our biggest inspiration to continue our business, let alone believe that we are always supported in becoming a part of your routine.
